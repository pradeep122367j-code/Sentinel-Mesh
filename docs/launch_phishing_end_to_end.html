Three words to teach first

  • Alert: a warning that something on the network looks odd.
  • Agent: a small AI worker with one job. Here: Triage (sorts), Investigation (explains with proof), Containment (proposes a fix).
  • Approval gate: the AI may never take a risky action alone. A human clicks Approve or Reject.

The story in the demo, in one breath

"An employee on the finance team clicked a phishing link. Their laptop, ws-fin-07, downloads the attacker's tool, then scans our file server fs-01, then someone uses stolen admin access from the IT jump host jump-01 to hammer the file server with passwords."

These two pages are in the left menu, separate from the scenarios. Launching a scenario does not change them. They answer the question every audience asks next: how good is the AI, and how do you know? Show them after the three stories.

Three ideas to teach first

  • Training: showing an AI many examples so it learns the pattern. "Epoch" means one full pass through the examples.
  • Loss: a score for how wrong the AI is while it learns. It should go down and then flatten.
  • Test split: examples kept hidden from the AI during training, used only afterwards to grade it. Good grades on hidden examples are the honest kind.
